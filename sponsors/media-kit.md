## Sponsorship is closed

Developer On Fire has finished, so sponsorship is closed. What follows is a record of what it was.

* Developer On Fire was a podcast about software developers - not programming itself, though that was often addressed - but about the people who make software.  The show explored life in the software world and developers shared stories, experiences, inspiration, and wisdom from their life in the world of writing code and designing and building systems.  It was about expressing the humanity of software geeks.
* An episode of the show published every week - on Mondays.
* The audience was a mix of software developers, testers, managers, and others interested in software using various platforms, languages, tools, and technology.
  * Each typical episode got roughly 2,000 to 2,500 downloads.  There were some with significantly more.
  * Episodes were downloaded in the range of 20,000-25,000 times per month.
* There were listeners all over the world.
* A sponsorship included:
  * Pre-roll spoken advertisement by show host in show audio, up to 60 seconds
  * 300 x 250 pixel graphical ad and up to 300 characters text in Show Notes Sidebar
