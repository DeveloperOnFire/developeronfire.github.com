Developer On Fire has finished, so it is no longer booking interviews. The show description and standard questions are kept here as a record.

Podcast information

Developer On Fire with Dave Rael was an interview podcast with inspiring and successful software professionals telling personal stories about their experiences with delivering value.  It was a chance for you to get to know your favorite geeks.

The idea of the show was to get guests to tell stories about their experiences in their software development careers and to share the insight and wisdom acquired. To this end, there was a set of standard questions. Not every episode featured all of the standard questions. This was not a robotic effort to get through the list. They were intended to get stories. If stories were being expressed without the standard questions, questions could be skipped. Generally, all episodes included a book recommendation and finished with 3 tips. The rest of the questions may or may not have made it in. This was, after all, a conversation and open to going in the direction that would best serve the listener.

Standard questions

  • What lights you up about technology and what you are currently doing?
  • How did you get started writing software?
  • Please share a story about time you failed.
  • Please share the story of your greatest success.  Make sure you share how this experience delivered value.
  • How do you stay current with what you need to know?
  • Book Recommendation
  • What has you the most excited about your current projects and future?
  • What are the greatest sources of pain in your life and work?
  • What do you geek out about other than software that really lights you up?
  • Can you make one prediction for the future of software?
  • 3 tips for delivering more value - they don't have to be programming or software related, but should facilitate delivering.
